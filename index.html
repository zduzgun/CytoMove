Desktop Alpha is the faster local path.

The web prototype is useful for quick review, but large images, multi-file groups, and ZIP exports feel better in the Windows Desktop Alpha. It runs the current Cytomove workflow locally and keeps the same privacy posture: assay images are not uploaded.

  • Full free alpha: no mandatory account or login.
  • Recommended for group analysis, plots, and full-resolution overlay exports.
  • Optional email feedback keeps you in the update loop before private beta.
