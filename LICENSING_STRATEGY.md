# Cytomove Licensing Strategy

Status: project licensing strategy.

Cytomove is intended to be released as source-available software for non-commercial
academic, educational, and research use. The preferred public template is
PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. Commercial use is not permitted under the
public research-use terms.

Commercial use includes, without limitation:

- integration into paid products, SaaS platforms, proprietary software, or
  commercial laboratory workflows;
- use in paid consulting, contract research, CRO/service deliverables, or
  commercial screening pipelines;
- redistribution, sublicensing, or hosting of Cytomove as part of a revenue-
  generating service.

Any commercial use requires a separate written commercial licence from the author
or rights holder.

Do not describe this model as OSI open source; use "source-available" or
"source-available for non-commercial academic and research use" in manuscripts,
repositories, and submission materials.

Repository release note: keep a root `LICENSE` file and public README language
consistent with this strategy. Commercial users must contact the author or rights
holder for a separate written commercial licence.
