Academic Access Terms

These terms describe the intended use of the Cytomove Academic Access, including demo access, registered academic access, and non-commercial use expectations.

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Release Status

Cytomove is provided as an academic access research tool. Features, measurements, user interface behavior, exports, and access rules may change as the project evolves.

Academic And Non-Commercial Use

The Academic Access is intended for academic, educational, and non-commercial research evaluation. Commercial use, commercial service delivery, paid consulting use, integration into commercial products, or use by commercial organizations requires a separate written licence.

Demo Access

Visitors may use the loginless demo to evaluate the workflow with local images, segmentation overlays, and core measurements. Full CSV, Excel, PNG, and ZIP exports require registered academic access.

Registered Access

Registered access may require email verification and manual review. Academic institutional email addresses, ORCID linkage if later enabled, institution, role, and intended use may be considered during review. Access may be approved, declined, limited, or withdrawn.

User Responsibilities

  • Use Cytomove only for lawful academic or non-commercial research evaluation unless a separate commercial licence is in place.
  • Do not upload, send, or share confidential patient data or personally identifiable clinical data through feedback or support channels.
  • Review all segmentation outputs before relying on measurements.
  • Do not attempt to bypass academic access controls or export restrictions.

Research Outputs

Cytomove outputs are intended to support reviewable image-analysis workflows. Users remain responsible for scientific interpretation, validation against their experimental context, and compliance with institutional or journal requirements.

Availability And Warranty

The Academic Access is provided without a service-level guarantee and without warranty. The tool may be unavailable, modified, or discontinued as the project evolves.

Privacy

Privacy information for the Academic Access is available in the Cytomove Privacy Policy.

Contact

For academic access, licensing, or terms questions, contact the Cytomove project team through the contact route listed on the main Cytomove website.

These access terms are a practical project notice for controlled academic evaluation. They are not a substitute for formal legal review where that is required.
