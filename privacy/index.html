Privacy Policy

Cytomove is a browser-based research tool for scratch wound healing assay analysis. The Academic Access is designed around a local-first workflow.

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Local Image Processing

When you use the Cytomove web app, assay images are processed in your browser. Cytomove does not upload your microscopy images to our server as part of the normal analysis workflow.

Account Information

If you sign in for the Academic Access, we may store account and access-review information through our authentication provider, including:

  • email address;
  • name and profile information provided by the sign-in provider;
  • institution, role, and intended use if you provide them;
  • academic access status, such as pending review or approved academic access.

Authentication Providers

Cytomove may use third-party authentication services, including Supabase and Google, to support Google sign-in and email magic-link sign-in. These providers process authentication data according to their own policies.

Feedback And Support

If you contact us, send feedback, or voluntarily share files for troubleshooting, we may use that information to respond to you, evaluate issues, and improve Cytomove. Please do not send confidential patient data or personally identifiable clinical data.

Research And Product Improvement

We may use aggregated, non-identifying usage and feedback information to improve Cytomove. We do not sell user account data or microscopy images.

Commercial Use

The Academic Access is intended for academic and non-commercial research evaluation. Commercial use requires a separate licence discussion.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Cytomove project team through the contact route listed on the main Cytomove website.

This page is a practical privacy notice for Cytomove. It is not a substitute for institution-specific legal review where that is required.
